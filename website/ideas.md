# NWCA Homepage Redesign — "The Circle" Design System
## Brief: 2030-era unified platform for wrestling coaches, athletes — men & women, freestyle & folkstyle

---

## Selected Approach: "The Circle" — Kinetic Wrestling Identity

**Design Movement:** Kinetic Sports Identity meets Modern Association Platform (Olympics.com × Rudis × The Athletic)

**Core Principles:**
1. The wrestling circle IS the brand — mat's concentric rings are the visual DNA of every section
2. Equal representation — men's and women's, freestyle and folkstyle share identical visual hierarchy
3. Platform consolidation — shop, news, events, rankings, membership all surface on one homepage
4. Forward motion — every element suggests movement, competition, progression

**Color Philosophy:**
- Arena Black `#0A0E1A` — the mat at night, deep and authoritative
- NWCA Navy `#17214B` — institutional authority, the association's core
- Championship Red `#C41E3A` — freestyle energy, women's wrestling power, action
- Chalk White `#F8F6F1` — clean, athletic, readable
- Burnished Gold `#B8860B` — rankings, championships, podium moments
- Steel Blue `#4A6FA5` — secondary accent, folkstyle/collegiate feel

**Layout Paradigm:**
- Asymmetric split hero: 60% full-bleed wrestling photography / 40% live content feed
- Horizontal rail navigation with mega-panel dropdowns
- Bento-grid content sections — varied card sizes like a modern dashboard
- Full-bleed "mat break" sections using overhead mat photography as section separators

**Signature Elements:**
1. Mat Circle Motif — red/blue/white concentric circles as section backgrounds, image frames, decorative overlays
2. Weight Class Numerals — large condensed weight class numbers (125, 133, 141...) as decorative typography
3. Scoreboard Strips — horizontal data strips styled like wrestling scoreboards for rankings

**Typography System:**
- Display: Bebas Neue — ultra-condensed, all-caps, zero compromise on impact
- Section Labels: Barlow Condensed SemiBold — athletic, readable
- Body: DM Sans Regular/Medium — modern, warm, accessible
- Data: Roboto Mono — scoreboard authenticity for rankings and stats

---

## Page Architecture (Scroll Order)

1. **Sticky Nav** — Logo | Coaching | Events | Rankings | Women's Wrestling | Store | Membership [CTA]
2. **Hero** — Full-bleed wrestling action (men + women alternating), mat circle overlay, headline + 5 quick-access tiles
3. **Platform Hub Strip** — Membership | Shop Gear | Convention | Career Center | Rankings
4. **News & Press Releases** — Bento editorial grid: 1 hero story + 4 supporting cards
5. **Upcoming Events** — Horizontal scroll rail with countdown timers + register CTAs
6. **Shop Portal** — Shopify-linked featured gear, 4 product cards + "Enter Store" CTA
7. **Publications Store** — Buy/download coaching guides, research papers, digital resources
8. **Coaching Development** — Three-tier cards: Youth | Scholastic | Collegiate
9. **Rankings Preview** — Scoreboard-style with division tabs (D1/D2/D3/Women)
10. **Grow the Sport** — Map teaser + participation stats
11. **WIBN Feature** — Wrestlers in Business Network spotlight
12. **Sponsors Strip** — Partner logos
13. **Footer** — Consolidated contact, links, social
